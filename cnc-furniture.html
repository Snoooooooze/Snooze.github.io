Designing CNC Manufacturable Furniture

A semester-long furniture design course where the only limit was your capabilities, constrained within the four pillars of furniture design.

Role
Product Designer
Timeline
August 2022 – December 2022

Design opportunity

A semester-long furniture design course where the only limit was your capabilities, constrained within the four pillars of furniture design:

  • Form: The visual element of the design and what makes it compelling within its intended space.
  • Function: The ergonomics, weight, and practical elements within the design.
  • Sustainability: The material usage is both sustainable and suitable for the product's lifecycle and environment.
  • Material usage: The design requires minimal waste of materials.
  1. Nested design to allow for maximum bed space area and minimal material usage
  2. Can be packed in flat pack packaging
  3. Requires no fasteners or screws, glue is optional

Final design

Process

Sketch

Like with most design, it starts with a sketch. Furniture design alongside CNC furniture design requires that the piece of furniture is well suited to the constraints of that approach to manufacturing.

Model

Once settled on a sketch, you go into modeling, where you design your sketch in 3D software and also generate the toolpath for the CNC router to understand your design specs.

Prototype

Before you get into your actual final design, you have to make multiple tests and iterations with scaling and practice materials to ensure your actual cut is not wasting material.

Prepare the wood

From prototyping you go into processing the wood, which in this case was walnut roughly 3 inches thick. I then used various tools and machinery to prepare the wood for CNC cutting.

Cut and assemble

After creating the prototype, I did some rough sizing over my actual walnut boards before embarking on making the final piece.

Reflection

This experience has showed me that designing something physical might be what I enjoy the most within design. UX means User Experience, and to me that encompasses everything around us.
