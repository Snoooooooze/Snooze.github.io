Designing the Next Generation of In-Vehicle Radio

Leading the UX design of Hyundai Motor Group's next-generation radio for North America, a modular system that has to work across many vehicle form factors.

This project is still underway and is not currently public. Here are some general renders of the greater systems the radio will be a part of.

Project background

Designing for vehicular interactions within the cockpit requires extreme intentionality, precision, and forethought for greater system-vehicle-user interactions. The primary objective is to deliver both a physical (steering controls) and software (infotainment) experience that is delightful while also being safe to use.

My task within this project is to lead the UX design for the next generation of Hyundai Motor Group's radio systems for the North American market, across all Hyundai, Genesis, and Kia vehicles being deployed with the next generation of infotainment systems.

These different vehicles each have unique form factors and, in some cases, manually adjustable form factors. That makes designing a modular system, one that reduces design and development time, an interesting design challenge.
